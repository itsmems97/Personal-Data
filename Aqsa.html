Ya Allah! Meri pyari Api Aqsa ko lambi, sehatmand aur barkat wali zindagi ata farma. Unhein hamesha apni hifazat mein rakh.

Unke shohar Ibrar ke saath unka rishta mohabbat, sukoon aur ek doosre ki samajh se bharpoor rakh.

Unke bete Abdul Hadi ko nek, ilm wala aur waalidain ki aankhon ki thandak bana. Use hamesha seedhe raaste par chalne ki taufeeq de.

Is ghar ko khushiyon, rizq mein barkat aur imaan ki roshni se bhar de. Har pareshani aur nazar-e-bad se door rakh.
